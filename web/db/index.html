How fast buses move

When buses are likely to be crowded

MTC does not publish ticket counts by hour, and the Chennai One app's crowd field is empty. What is real and hourly: when the city travels — Chennai Metro's ticket entries by hour — and how many buses are on the road. Their ratio says when there are the fewest buses per traveller. An estimate, not a passenger count.

Single bus

Any one bus over the days we have archived: where it ran, how far and for how long, from its own GPS fixes. These are observed GPS kilometres for buses that reported — not MTC's planned, scheduled or ticketed kilometres, which are not published. A bus is seen every 10–15 minutes, gaps over 40 minutes count nothing, and buses that stop reporting vanish, so a day's figure is a floor. The archive starts on the evening of 19 Oct 2026.

How this is collected. A collector polls the official MTC app's live feed (Chalo) route by route at 2 requests a second — until 20 Oct 2026 it read Chennai One — and archives each bus's GPS fix; this page is rebuilt from that archive every hour. Speeds and bunching need two fixes of the same bus, a few minutes apart. Buses that do not report GPS are not counted.
